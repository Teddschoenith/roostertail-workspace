I - The Venue

A room with a point of view.

Since 1958, the Roostertail has been part of Detroit's Motown-era story. In the Palm River Room, floor-to-ceiling windows bring the river into the evening and give the benefit a clear sense of place.

A Detroit room for a meaningful gathering

II - The Evening

Gather. Dine. Give.

Guests arrive at 6:00PM for an evening centered on the Epilepsy Foundation benefit. The four-hour Package 4 program includes open bar service and a plated or buffet dinner.

The Palm River Room offers a natural setting for reception, dinner, remarks, and time together, with the river visible through the room's windows.

The room can be arranged to support the benefit's program while keeping dinner and conversation at the center of the night.

III - The Table

Package 4

Package 4 includes open bar service, a plated or buffet dinner, cake cutting, and a four-hour event timeframe.

Dinner

Choice of Caesar or exotic greens salad, chicken piccata, Roostertail stuffed chicken, or parmesan-dusted airline chicken, with vegan stuffed bell pepper available. Dinner includes garlic whipped mashed or redskin potatoes and crisp green beans.

For buffet service, the meal also includes spice-rubbed top sirloin and a pasta or lasagna second entrée.

Open Bar

The 3.5-hour open bar includes Nue Vodka, Valeo Tequila, Bacardi, New Amsterdam Gin, Canadian Club, Dewars White Label, and Jim Beam, plus Budweiser, Bud Light, Chardonnay, Moscato, Rosé, and Cabernet.
