# Roostertail Packages — quick reference

Reference only. Live pricing lives in RCMS; if these numbers ever disagree with RCMS or the rep, the rep wins. Full packets: `wedding-packages.pdf` and `special-events-packages.pdf` in this folder, also hosted at go.roostertail.com/wedding-packages.pdf and go.roostertail.com/special-events-packages.pdf.

## Special Events Packages
Corporate, holiday, galas, proms and school events, birthdays, showers, religious, graduations.
All include a 4-hour event, 3.5-hour beverage service, and cake cutting. 6% sales tax AND 24% gratuity are ADDED. Venue rental added.

| Package | Per person | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $69 | Coffee/tea/water + stationed hot appetizers (choice of 4) |
| 2 | $75 | Coffee/tea/water + plated or buffet dinner |
| 3 | $91 | Open bar + stationed hot appetizers (choice of 4) |
| 4 | $99 | Open bar + plated or buffet dinner |

Math: food = rate x guests. Gratuity = 24% of food. Sales tax = 6% of (food PLUS gratuity), never food alone. Then add venue rental and flat items.

## Wedding Packages
Per-person price INCLUDES tax and gratuity. All include champagne toast (head + cake table), venue coordinator, dietary accommodations, cake cutting. Children and vendors $75 each. Halal $5 pp.

| Package | Per person | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $149 | 4.5-hr reception, 4-hr open House bar, plated or buffet dinner |
| 2 | $179 | 5-hr reception, 4.5-hr open Deluxe bar, Starter hors d'oeuvres, third buffet entree |
| 3 | $219 | 5-hr reception, 4.5-hr open Top Shelf bar, Romance hors d'oeuvres, dual plated dinner, late night snack |

Ceremony sites (immediately followed by reception): The Patio (Marine Room) $2,750. The Deck (Palm River Room) $3,850.
Venue rental: sliding scale by package rate, guest count, date, and room; typically $4,500 to $9,500. The rep gives each event's exact number.

Deposits: $5,000 Marine Room / Palm River Room, $1,000 Club. Non-refundable.
