I · The Venue

Sixty-six years on
the Detroit River.

Since 1958 the Roostertail has been Detroit's waterfront room, from Motown's golden era to today's most extravagant nights. Floor-to-ceiling windows put the river and the skyline behind every table, and our team has spent decades making evenings like this one feel effortless.

When we host an event, it is not just another night. We shape the evening around your people, then handle every detail from dinner service to production.

An evening for the people

The night opens with cocktails and passed appetizers as guests arrive, moves into chef-attended food stations, pauses for Delta Dental's remarks and employee recognition, and finishes with music and dancing over the river.
